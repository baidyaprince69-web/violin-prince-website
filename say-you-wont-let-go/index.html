THE STORY IN THE SOUND

A melody that feels close to home.

Originally performed by English singer-songwriter James Arthur, “Say You Won't Let Go” became known for its intimate, storytelling character. Here, the melody gets a different voice: the violin.

Some songs do not need a huge arrangement to feel meaningful. Their power comes from the sense that someone is speaking honestly, without trying to make the moment bigger than it is. This song has that quiet, personal quality — a love ballad built around closeness, commitment, and the passing of time.

A violin interpretation changes the way that story is told. Instead of words, the listener follows the shape of the melody: the rise of a phrase, the softness of a held note, the breath between one idea and the next. The emotion is left open enough for each person to bring their own memories to it.

This page is a little listening room for that feeling. Settle in, play the cover, and let the melody speak in its own way.

LISTEN A LITTLE CLOSER

From a voice to four strings.

A familiar song can feel different when the lead vocal melody is carried by an instrument. These are listening notes, not claims about a particular recording technique.

01 / THE MELODY

A human-shaped line

The violin can bend into a phrase, lean on a note, and let a melodic line feel almost like a voice without using words.

02 / THE SPACE

Room to breathe

Pauses and sustained notes give the listener time to absorb the emotion instead of filling every moment with sound.

03 / THE MEMORY

Your own meaning

Instrumental music leaves space for personal interpretation: a person, a place, or a moment that the melody brings back.

A NOTE FROM THE ARTIST

Hello, I'm Prince.

I'm Prince Baidya, the violinist behind Violin Prince. I create violin covers of melodies that stay with me — from English songs to Bengali favourites, Bollywood music, and Rabindra Sangeet.

For this cover, the goal is simple: give a familiar melody another way to reach the listener. Thank you for spending a little of your time with my music and supporting an independent student musician's creative journey.
