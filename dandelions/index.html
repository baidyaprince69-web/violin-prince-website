The feeling

Some wishes are made of music.

There is something delicate about this melody: it can feel bright without being loud, romantic without needing grand gestures, and nostalgic without losing its warmth. The violin gives the tune room to breathe.

A melody, reimagined

A little wistful.
A little hopeful.

This version invites you to listen to the shape of the melody itself: the way a note can linger, how a phrase can rise gently, and how a familiar tune can feel different when it is carried by one instrument.

No lyrics are needed here. Just a melody, a bow, and the feeling you bring to it.

“A quiet melody can say what a thousand words cannot.”

Behind the song

Dandelions, a song about hope and longing.

Canadian singer-songwriter Ruth B. released “Dandelions” as part of her 2017 album Safe Haven. Its dreamy pop character and wishful emotional tone make the melody especially suited to an instrumental reading.

In a violin cover, the melody steps away from the original vocal and becomes a line of its own. Each phrase can feel like a thought held for a moment before it drifts away.

♪ Original song: Ruth B. · Safe Haven (2017) ↗

What to listen for

The melody in the foreground

Listen for the contours of the tune, its gentle lifts, and the spaces between phrases. The violin makes those little turns of the melody the centre of the experience.

One instrument, a new colour

Without the original vocal, the listener can imagine their own story in the notes. That is part of the joy of instrumental covers: the music stays familiar, while the feeling becomes personal.
