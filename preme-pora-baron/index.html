The story behind the song

The song behind the feeling.

“Preme Pora Baron” is from Sweater, the 2019 Bengali film. Lagnajita Chakraborty sings it; Ranajoy Bhattacharjee wrote and composed the song. The title itself sets up a contrast between falling in love and being told not to.

In the film, the song is part of Tuku’s story. Learning to knit becomes one step in a much bigger change: she gradually gains confidence and starts to see herself differently.

That contrast gives the song its character. It is romantic, but not without hesitation; the melody carries both affection and the pressure of expectation.

Directed by Shieladitya Moulik, Sweater follows Tuku as she gradually gains confidence. The film is based on Joeeta Sengupta’s short story Wool Kaata.

The musical identity

The melody and the original recording.

The original recording is led by Lagnajita Chakraborty’s vocal. The song was composed and written by Ranajoy Bhattacharjee for Sweater.

The original recording also features strings: Rohan Roy is credited on violin and Raja Chowdhury on guitar. Ranajoy Bhattacharjee is credited for the composition, lyrics, arrangement, programming and music production. This cover brings the song to the violin.

In a violin cover, the melody takes the lead. Listen for the main tune, the returning phrases and the way the song moves without its original vocal.
