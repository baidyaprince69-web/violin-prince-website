A LITTLE ABOUT ME

The violin is how I tell the story.

I'm Prince Baidya, an independent violinist sharing instrumental interpretations of the songs that move me. My musical world brings together Bengali favourites, Rabindra Sangeet, Bollywood melodies, English pop and familiar tunes from different places.

Violin Prince is a space for those songs to be heard in a different way — with a little nostalgia, a little emotion, and the sound of the strings at the centre.
