Behind the song

How a timeless melody
came to life.

“Kal Ho Naa Ho” is the title song of the 2003 Hindi film. Its reflective message sits at the heart of the story: life is uncertain, so the moments we have deserve to be lived fully.

The song

A melody built around feeling

The title track was composed by Shankar–Ehsaan–Loy, with lyrics by Javed Akhtar and vocals by Sonu Nigam. Within the film, it returns at emotional turning points, helping connect the characters’ changing hopes, love, and understanding of life.

How it began

From a tune in Pune to a classic

In a 2024 interview with The Hollywood Reporter India, the composers recalled gathering in Pune to develop the film’s music. Shankar Mahadevan remembered the title melody coming together in about nine minutes; Ehsaan Noorani said Javed Akhtar took around three weeks to craft lyrics that would match its simplicity. Sonu Nigam later chose to record his vocal again, simplifying it to better serve the song.
