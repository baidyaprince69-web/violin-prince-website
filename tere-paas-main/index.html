The idea of a cover

A familiar song.
A different voice.

VIOLINVPINSTRUMENTAL

A cover gives a performer a way to revisit music that already exists. The original song has its own contributors and recording; a violin cover approaches the melody through an instrumental performance.

This page keeps those two things distinct. The song credits belong to the original work, while the embedded video is the performance by Prince Baidya. The aim here is simple: give the violin performance a dedicated place where listeners can watch, listen, and share.

The visual design follows the supplied thumbnail: its cool turquoise centre is balanced by the warmth of amber and champagne. Those colours carry through the page so the artwork, typography, and player feel connected.

Is this the original “Tere Paas Main” recording?

No. This page embeds the violin cover by Prince Baidya. The original song has its own performers and rights holders.

Who composed the original song?

The supplied cover’s credits identify A. R. Rahman as the composer, Deepali Sahay for the original vocals, and Irshad Kamil for the lyrics. The song is associated with Main Vaapas Aaunga.
