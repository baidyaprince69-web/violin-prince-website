A Christmas carol already carries a history of its own. This performance gives the melody space to speak through the tone and phrasing of the violin.

This page is a home for my violin performance of Hark the Herald Angels Sing. The intention is simple: let a familiar Christmas melody take another shape through an instrumental voice.

I’m Prince Baidya, making music as Violin Prince. I explore melodies from English songs, Bollywood and Bengali music through the violin. Each cover is a new way to listen to something you may already know.

Take your time with the performance. The music is here whenever you need a quieter moment.
