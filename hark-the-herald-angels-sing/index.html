01 / Behind the melody

A little winter light,
between the notes.

Some melodies arrive with a feeling already attached to them. This carol brings a sense of celebration and reflection; played on violin, its familiar tune gets room to breathe through tone, phrasing, and the space between notes.

This page is a home for my violin performance of Hark the Herald Angels Sing. The aim is simple: to let a familiar Christmas melody speak in an instrumental voice and give you a calm place to listen.

I’m Prince Baidya, making music as Violin Prince. I explore melodies from English songs, Bollywood, and Bengali music through the violin. Each cover is a chance to listen again to a song you may already know, and to notice a different colour in its melody.

Settle in, turn the volume to a comfortable level, and enjoy the performance in your own way.
